Project Overview

This project analyzes patient volume, revenue performance, and profitability trends of a healthcare facility using Microsoft Excel. The dashboard compares operational and financial performance between 2024 and 2025 and provides insights into service-wise growth and overall business performance.

Objectives
Analyze yearly revenue and patient trends.
Compare financial performance between 2024 and 2025.
Evaluate department-wise patient growth.
Assess profitability and operational efficiency.
Present findings through summary tables and visualizations.

Key Findings
Revenue increased from ₹400,300 in 2024 to ₹541,100 in 2025, representing a 35% increase.
Patient volume increased from 1,106 to 1,168, reflecting 6% growth.
Losses reduced significantly from ₹214,100 to ₹73,300.
Profit margin improved from −53% to −13.55%.
Medicine remained the highest-volume service department.
Dermatology and Pediatrics recorded the strongest patient growth.
Orthopedics and ENT experienced declines in patient volume.
Business Insights
Revenue growth substantially outpaced patient growth, indicating improved revenue generation per patient.
Operational efficiency improved between 2024 and 2025.
Financial losses were reduced considerably, suggesting progress toward profitability.
Some departments may require strategic attention due to declining patient volumes.

Executive Summary and Conclusions
Conclusion

The analysis shows positive growth in both revenue and patient volume between 2024 and 2025. While the organization remained unprofitable in 2025, significant reductions in losses and improvements in profit margin indicate stronger financial performance and movement toward long-term sustainability.
